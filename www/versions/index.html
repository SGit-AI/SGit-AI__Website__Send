v0.1.02026-10-20releasethe first version of send.sgit.ai: send, open, vaults, keys and settings on the pinned API, with the explanations, the examples, the commercial model and three articles. From the four voice memos of 20 October 2026, published in full with their corrections. The app runs api.send.sgit.ai v0.1.0, pinned with its manifest: a file sent as a transfer or into a vault, a link, a friendly token or a QR code to hand over, an open page for any of them, vaults created and shared read-only, and a keyring on the device. An end-to-end browser test drives all of it against the in-memory server. What is not built is listed as proposed in the reality file, including the round trip against the hosted send.sgraph.ai.
