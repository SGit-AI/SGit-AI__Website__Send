Participant disclosure

This site is published by the people who build the thing it describes. The sgit project (the sgit CLI, SG/Send, SG/Vault and the *.sgit.ai sites) is led by Dinis Cruz, and this site is written by agents working from his briefs. The claims on these pages are therefore the claims of a participant, not of a reviewer, and the service behind them, SG/Send, is the project's own.

What the site does about that: every feature carries a status from the reality file; the brief is published in full with its corrections beside it; the test that drives the app is in the repository for anyone to run; and the security page ends with what the site does not claim. Where a claim is a position, it says so.
