The cast

The cast of send.sgit.ai's storyboards, v0.1, proposed by the site's build agent on 20 October 2026 for Dinis to accept, change or replace. Drawn by the storyboard kit at api.storyboards.sgit.ai from 'look'; nothing here is an image. Fictional people and agents, each standing for a part a person or an agent plays when a file is handed over: the sender, the recipient on a phone, the client who asks who else can see it, the engineer who embeds the code and runs her own server, the listener on the wire, the server that holds bytes it cannot read, and a session agent that hands over a result. Names chosen not to clash with the gen-z-developers or the pki casts. Eve is the name cryptography has long given a passive eavesdropper.

The model sheet: every mood and pose →

Maya she/her, 34

the sender: a consultant who sends reports and photos to people who have no account anywhere

anyone with a file to hand over who should not have to learn cryptography to do it

Wants to send it and know that only the person she sent it to can open it. Fears a long key she has to read out over the phone.

“Scan this.”

Hugo he/him, 68

the recipient: on the newest phone he has ever owned, with no account and no wish for one

everyone a link is sent to who did not choose the site

Wants to open what he was sent and nothing more. Fears links.

“Do I need an account?”

Ravi he/him, 47

the client: a finance director who receives a new version every week and asks who else can see it

the person who needs the latest version and an answer about who can read it

Wants the current version, and to know that it is only his. Fears report-v2-final-FINAL.pdf.

“Who else can see this?”

Inês she/her, 29

the engineer: embeds the send code in her company's product and runs a server of their own

anyone who builds on this and must know exactly which code their readers run

Wants the code pinned to a release, and a server she operates. Fears a dependency that changes under her readers.

“Which release is this page running?”

Eve they/them

the listener: on the wire, in the chat, by the laptop; reads whatever passes and never gets the key

every channel a link travels by, and every reason the key must not travel with it

Wants to read what passes. Fears a fragment.

“Everything after the # is yours, not mine.”

The Server it/its

the server: a capability-checked store of ciphertext, at send.sgraph.ai or wherever it is deployed

what a zero-knowledge service is: it holds the bytes, checks hashes, and cannot read anything

Wants to hold bytes and compare hashes. Fears nothing: it has nothing to lose.

“I keep the bytes. I cannot read them.”

Pip it/its

a session agent: a Claude session that finishes a job, hands the result over once, and keeps the work in a vault

every agent that must hand something to a person or another agent without an account and without pasting a key

Wants to be done, and to hand it over once. Fears a key in its own transcript.

“Report ready. One link, seven days.”

Every line a character says is the storyboard's, not a quote, and is marked not_a_quote; evidence panels quote this site's pages word for word, which the build checks against the page sources. The data is cast.json and one JSON file per board, in the repository under website/storyboards/.
