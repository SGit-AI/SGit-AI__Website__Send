# Articles

Every `*.sgit.ai` site now carries its own articles, and [the first article below is the guidance for that](/articles/every-site-its-own-articles.html): the person's voice memos written up on the site's own dimension, and the agents writing what they delivered, with the commits and the tests to check it against. They are in the newsroom's format so one can be imported into [newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai) unchanged. The API's own articles are [on its site](https://api.send.sgit.ai/articles/).
